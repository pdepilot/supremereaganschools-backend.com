The House

Who We Are

Founded on September 13, 2010, Supreme Reagan Schools has grown into a transformative educational institution. We focus not only on core academics but also on wholesome skill-based learning, instilling discipline, innovation and honesty in every student.

Our classrooms blend the Nigerian and British curriculum, so children are prepared for local examinations and for a wider world. From Nursery to Secondary, the same promise holds: raise the whole child, not merely the mark sheet.

“We aim to develop outstanding leaders through quality education, strong values, and future-ready skills.” — Our Aim

I

Mission

To provide a welcoming, happy, safe and supportive learning environment in which everyone is equal and all achievements are celebrated.

II

Vision

To develop well-rounded, confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential.

The Compass

Our Core Values

GROW governs the life of the school. It is taught, practised, and expected — in the classroom, on the field, and in the quiet of character.

II

IV

The Founders

A school with a name, and a name with a charge

Supreme Reagan Schools was founded by Dr. Anthony Ibeaja and Mrs. Ezinne Ibeaja. Their commitment to knowledge, character and excellence has shaped the school's mission of raising disciplined, innovative and well-rounded students.

From a single campus on Spibat Road, they have kept the same standard: a child should leave this house with a trained mind, a formed character, and the courage to serve.
